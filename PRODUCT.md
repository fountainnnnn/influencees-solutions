# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Singapore brands evaluating creators and campaigns, and Singapore creators seeking discovery, credible profiles, and clearer collaboration opportunities. Visitors should quickly understand which path is relevant to them and what Influencees can help them do.

## Product Purpose

Influencees is a trust and intelligence layer for creator marketing in Singapore. The public site explains creator discovery, profile and performance data, Trust Check, campaign workflows, rate guidance, reporting, and Ai-kyo while directing brands and creators toward the right next step.

## Brand Personality

Confident, clear, and locally grounded. The experience should feel polished and capable without becoming corporate, overly technical, or inflated. It should balance product credibility with the energy of Singapore's creator community.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS card grids, excessive glass effects, decorative icons, vague AI claims, duplicated feature lists, and pages that feel assembled without a clear hierarchy. Do not reproduce the fragmented or “vibe-coded” qualities of the current public site.

## Design Principles

- Show the product through credible visuals and specific outcomes.
- Give brands and creators distinct, concise journeys.
- Prioritize trust, explain what data and checks actually mean.
- Distinguish live, beta, and upcoming capabilities honestly.
- Reduce repetition so each section earns its place.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic structure, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, responsive layouts, descriptive image text, and reduced-motion alternatives. Avoid interactions that require precise pointer control or motion to be understood.
